Smart Land‑to‑Home — conceptual planning prototype.
Smart Land-to-Home is a preliminary conceptual planning and visualization system. Generated layouts are not construction-ready architectural or structural drawings. Final room dimensions, setbacks, floor count, structural safety, fire safety, electrical, plumbing and local building-code compliance must be verified by qualified professionals and the relevant authorities before construction.
